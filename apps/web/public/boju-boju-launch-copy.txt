LONG POST / COMMUNITY POST

Most hide-and-seek games ask you to find a good hiding spot. BOJU BOJU Arena asks a harder question: can you become the spot?

Chameleons paint their body, sample nearby surfaces, pick one of 14 poses, then Lock to blend into the scene. The better your paint coverage, color match, pose, and stillness, the stronger your camouflage — and the harder you are to shimmer out.

Hunters get a gun, limited ammo, spot pings, and a sharp eye. Missed shots cost ammo and slow you down. Decoys look like players but waste bullets. Bold hides inside a hunter's line of sight earn more survival points.

What is in the playable build:
- 5 original maps: market, warehouse, rooftops, garden, arcade
- 14 hider poses and 3 body sizes
- Body painting with brush, fill, eyedropper, undo/redo, and paint completeness
- World color sampling so your body can match nearby props
- Lock/Blend system with a visible blend score
- Painted decoys, whistles, shimmer tells, and line-of-sight scoring
- Gamer profiles, stats, room codes, quick match, and spectators
- Desktop and touch controls
